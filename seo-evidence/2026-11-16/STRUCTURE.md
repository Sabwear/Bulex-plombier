# Sitemap structure

/sitemap.xml remains one Next.js XML sitemap, comfortably below the 50,000-URL limit. It contains 126 published canonical Dutch/English URLs as of 16 November 2026: 34 main pages, 16 services, 6 solutions, 38 city pages and 32 advice pages. Counts increase automatically when scheduled guides publish.

Dutch uses unprefixed paths; English uses /en. Every URL has reciprocal nl-BE, en-BE and Dutch x-default alternate links. Internal rewrites, API URLs, legacy redirects, future posts and area pages failing the bilingual local-content gate are excluded.

Owner override: all published areas are now included, giving 178 current entries: 34 main pages, 16 services, 6 solutions, 90 city pages and 32 advice pages. This supersedes the earlier 126-entry profile-gated sitemap.
